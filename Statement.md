# Statement

Problem Statement

People who want to watch a lot of movies often struggle to keep track of what they still need to watch, what they've already seen, and what they thought of it. Notes get scattered across notebooks, messaging apps, or memory, making it hard to revisit past opinions or plan what to watch next. This project provides a simple, centralized place to manage a personal movie watchlist and viewing history.

 Scope of the Project

The project is a personal movie watchlist and review web app. It covers:
- Creating a user account and logging in
- Adding movies to a personal watchlist
- Marking movies as "To Watch" or "Watched"
- Rating and reviewing watched movies
- Viewing basic analytics on genre preferences and watch history

It does not cover fetching movie data from external movie databases, social features (sharing/following other users), or streaming/playback of any kind.

Target Users

- Casual and avid movie watchers who want a simple way to track what to watch next
- Users who like rating and reviewing movies for their own reference
- Anyone who wants a lightweight, personal alternative to spreadsheets or notes apps for tracking movies
